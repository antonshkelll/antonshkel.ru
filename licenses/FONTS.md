| `jetbrainsmono.woff2` | JetBrains Mono | Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono) |
| `cormorant.woff2`, `cormorant-italic.woff2` | Cormorant Garamond | Copyright 2015 The Cormorant Project Authors (https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant) |
| `onest.woff2` | Onest | Copyright 2021 The Onest Project Authors (https://github.com/simpals/onest) |
| `sofiasans.woff2`, `sofiasansxc.woff2` | Sofia Sans, Sofia Sans Extra Condensed | Copyright 2019 The Sofia Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/lettersoup/Sofia-Sans) |

`glyphs.woff2` — подмножество Inter Tight: знак ₽, стрелки, №, ², ³, ✓ и ×. Оно подстраховывает шрифты, в которых этих символов нет.
